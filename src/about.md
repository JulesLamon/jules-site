I grew up in the Tampa Bay area and was homeschooled, which gave me the room to move quickly. I started taking college courses through Hillsborough Community College at 13, joined the Phi Theta Kappa honor society, and finished my Associate in Arts before I arrived at New College of Florida, where I hold the Presidential Scholarship.

At New College I'm completing a double Area of Concentration in **English** and **Political Science** on a pre-law track. The two fit together better than people expect: both are about reading closely, building an argument, and asking who has power over whose story. My senior thesis will bring them together, starting with a tutorial on law and literature in Samuel Richardson's *Clarissa*.

Sports have been the other half of my education. I've competed in basketball, cross country, soccer, volleyball, gymnastics, figure skating, dance, and flag football, and I've run a 15K. Coaches tend to describe me with one word: *versatile*. I've played every position on the basketball floor, and in one season I went from sixth man to starter and team leader, because I learn the game plan quickly and try to keep everyone around me motivated. In my first-ever cross-country season, as a senior, my teammates and coach voted me the team's most influential member.

I'm not afraid to get my hands dirty, either. Homeschooling meant real projects: flooring, plumbing, painting, and working on my own car, from oil changes to a suspension upgrade.

**What I'm working toward:** law school, with a strong interest in sports law and Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) policy. Athletes, especially those who come up outside a traditional high school program, face real questions about eligibility, contracts, and ownership of their own names. I want to help answer them.
