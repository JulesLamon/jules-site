## Law and Literature: Samuel Richardson's *Clarissa*
*Independent research tutorial with Prof. John Park · New College of Florida · Fall 2026 – January 2027*

I'm researching how eighteenth-century legal ideas of consent and criminality shape *Clarissa*, one of the longest novels in English. This term's work, about 7,000 words, is the groundwork for an integrated English–Political Science senior thesis, and I'm continuing it in a January independent study on consent and John Locke.
