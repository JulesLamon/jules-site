## New College of Florida Women's Basketball (NAIA)
*Sarasota, Florida · 2025 – present*

Recruited since my junior year of high school. I balance practices, film sessions, and travel with a double-major course load. I received the team's Hardworking Award in October 2025, and I take part in team community service.

## Before college
*FISH homeschool athletics (TBCAL) · AAU: Tampa Bay Kings, then Bounty Hunters*

- **Varsity basketball from 7th grade.** I played every position. In one season I went from sixth man to starter and team leader by mastering the game plan first and lifting my teammates. Team MVP as an 8th grader.
- **AAU State Showdown champion and tournament MVP** with the Bounty Hunters (2024), playing almost every minute of four back-to-back games.
- **NCAA College Basketball Academy (2024):** one of 300 players chosen nationally, competing in front of Division I, II, and III coaches.
- **Senior season:** 11.9 points, 8.0 rebounds, and 2.3 steals per game.
- **Cross country:** voted most influential team member by teammates and coach in my first season, as a senior.
- **Soccer:** ranked third in Florida as a goalkeeper my senior year, and I scored whenever I was moved to offense.
- **Also:** volleyball, gymnastics, figure skating, dance, flag football, and a 15K race.

## Service

- **NCF Women's Basketball:** team service, including staffing a state debate tournament hosted at New College.
